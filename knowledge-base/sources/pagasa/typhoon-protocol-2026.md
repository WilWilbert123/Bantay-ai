# DOST-PAGASA Tropical Cyclone & Typhoon Safety Guidelines 2026

## Overview of Typhoon Signal Warnings
- Signal No. 1: Tropical cyclone winds of 39-61 km/h expected within 36 hours.
- Signal No. 2: Winds of 62-88 km/h expected within 24 hours. Preemptive evacuation for coastal areas.
- Signal No. 3: Winds of 89-117 km/h expected within 18 hours. Major risk to light structures and storm surge up to 3m.
- Signal No. 4: Winds of 118-184 km/h expected within 12 hours. Severe destruction to structures.
- Signal No. 5: Winds over 185 km/h. Catastrophic damage.

## Required Preparation
1. Prepare a 72-Hour Survival Go-Bag containing non-perishable food, 3 gallons of clean water per person, flashlight, radio, first aid kit, and essential medications.
2. Secure roofs, doors, and window shutters. Clean drainage systems around the house.
3. Charge all mobile devices, emergency lights, and power banks.

## Safety During Typhoon Landfall
1. Stay indoors in a sturdy structure. Keep away from windows.
2. Turn off the main electric breaker if floodwater enters the house.
3. Do not venture outside during the passage of the typhoon eye when weather temporarily clears.
