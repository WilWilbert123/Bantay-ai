# PHIVOLCS Earthquake & Volcanic Eruption Safety Manual 2026

## Earthquake Action Guide
- DUCK, COVER, & HOLD: Drop to your hands and knees, cover your head under a sturdy desk or table, and hold on until shaking stops.
- Stay clear of glass windows, tall cabinets, and unanchored objects.
- Evacuate via stairs when shaking stops. NEVER USE ELEVATORS.
- Watch out for aftershocks and fallen power lines.

## Volcanic Alert Levels & Ashfall Protection
- Alert Level 0: Quiet / Normal.
- Alert Level 1: Low-level unrest.
- Alert Level 2: Moderate unrest.
- Alert Level 3: High-level unrest / Magmatic intrusion. Evacuate 7km danger zone.
- Alert Level 4: Hazardous eruption imminent.
- Alert Level 5: Hazardous eruption in progress.

## Ashfall Guidelines
- Wear N95 masks or wet cloth over nose and mouth.
- Keep doors and windows closed to prevent ash infiltration.
- Protect eyes with goggles and avoid driving in heavy ashfall due to reduced visibility.
