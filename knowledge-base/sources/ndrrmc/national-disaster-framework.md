# NDRRMC National Disaster Framework & Guidelines 2026

## Nationwide Emergency Hotline
- **National Emergency Hotline:** 911
- **NDRRMC Operations Center:** (02) 8911-5061 to 65

## General Disaster Management and Response
1. **Coordination**: NDRRMC acts as the central node for coordinating disaster response at a national level. Follow immediate instructions issued via emergency text alerts.
2. **Community Evacuation**: Pay attention to your Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (LDRRMO) for preemptive and forced evacuation orders. 
3. **Suspension of Classes and Work**: Follow the localized cancellation rules based on PAGASA warning signals or local chief executive declarations.

## Tsunami Guidelines
- Immediately move to higher ground (at least 10 meters above sea level) if you are near the coast and feel a strong earthquake.
- Do not wait for an official tsunami warning if shaking is severe.
- Stay away from the beach. Never go down to the shore to watch a tsunami come in.
