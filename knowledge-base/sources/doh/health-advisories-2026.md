# Department of Health (DOH) Post-Disaster Health Advisories 2026

## Prevention of Water-borne Illnesses (WASH)
- **Safe Drinking Water**: Always boil water for 3 minutes or use chlorination/water purification tablets if the local water supply is disrupted.
- **Sanitation**: Use designated portalets or temporary latrines in evacuation centers to prevent the spread of cholera, typhoid, and dysentery.
- **Hand Hygiene**: Wash hands with soap and clean water regularly, especially before eating and after using the toilet.

## Outbreak Management in Evacuation Centers
- **Respiratory Infections**: Wear face masks in crowded areas to prevent the spread of influenza, COVID-19, and tuberculosis.
- **Skin Diseases**: Keep the skin dry. If you develop rashes or wounds, immediately seek the medical desk in the evacuation center for antibiotic ointments.

## Mental Health and Psychosocial Support (MHPSS)
- Disasters can cause acute stress, anxiety, or trauma. 
- The DOH, alongside MSWDO, provides psychosocial processing stations. 
- If experiencing hyperventilation, panic attacks, or severe distress, approach the nearest health worker for immediate intervention.
- DOH National Crisis Hotline: 1553
