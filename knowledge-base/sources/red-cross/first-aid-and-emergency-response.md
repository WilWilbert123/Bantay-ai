# Philippine Red Cross First Aid & Emergency Response 2026

## Basic First Aid Guidelines
1. **Assess the Situation**: Ensure the area is safe for you and the victim before providing aid. 
2. **Bleeding**: Apply direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth. Elevate the injured area above the heart if possible.
3. **Burns**: Cool the burn under running water for at least 10 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments. Loosely cover with a sterile bandage.
4. **Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR)**: If the person is unresponsive and not breathing, call emergency services and begin chest compressions at a rate of 100-120 per minute.

## Post-Disaster Health Risks
- **Leptospirosis**: Avoid wading in floodwaters. If exposure is unavoidable, wash thoroughly with soap and water immediately, and consult a doctor for prophylaxis (Doxycycline) within 24-72 hours.
- **Dengue Prevention**: Empty all stagnant water containers. Use mosquito repellents and wear long sleeves.
- **Water Safety**: Boil drinking water for at least 3 minutes if the water supply is compromised, or use water purification tablets.
