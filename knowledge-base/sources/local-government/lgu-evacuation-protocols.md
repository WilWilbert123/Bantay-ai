# Local Government Unit (LGU) Evacuation & Community Protocols

## Designated Evacuation Centers
- **Primary Shelters**: Public schools, multi-purpose barangay halls, and covered courts are the standard evacuation centers.
- **Coordination**: Register with the barangay desk officer upon arriving at an evacuation center to ensure you receive relief goods and medical assistance.

## Preemptive vs. Forced Evacuation
- **Preemptive Evacuation**: Initiated when a hazard is imminent. Residents are strongly advised to leave voluntarily for designated shelters.
- **Forced Evacuation**: Declared when the threat is critical and life-threatening. Local authorities and police will enforce mandatory evacuation. Refusal to evacuate may result in penalties or forced removal to ensure safety.

## Local Emergency Resources
- Keep a list of your specific Barangay Emergency Response Team (BERT) contact numbers.
- Relief distribution is managed via the local Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO/CSWDO). Do not hoard supplies; rations are calculated per family.
